Background Tracking & Battery Guide

Why Background Tracking Needs Attention

Mobile operating systems (Android & iOS) aggressively terminate background processes to save battery. To ensure your travel route is honestly recorded for reimbursement:

🤖 Android Devices:
  1. Go to Settings > Apps > Map Notes (or Chrome).
  2. Tap Battery and select "Unrestricted" (or disable "Battery Optimization").
  3. On Xiaomi/Samsung: Enable "Autostart" and lock the app in the recent apps tray.
🍎 iPhone / iOS:
  1. In Safari, tap the Share button and choose "Add to Home Screen".
  2. During your shift, keep the app active or enable the built-in Screen Wake Lock below so the screen remains on while driving between venues.
